# Surface Brief: Landing Page (app/page.tsx)

## Scope and Mode
- Surface: Landing Page (`app/page.tsx`)
- Visitor Mode: Persuade
- Target Audience: Indonesian & Southeast Asian fantasy authors, fiction novel writers, and tabletop RPG Game Masters.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The page refuses the category-default template of a generic marketing hero over three identical static feature cards. Instead, it proves its power directly: an interactive "Living Scriptorium Desk" where the visitor witnesses and tests EPOS's live worldbuilding engine—real-time `@mention` entity linking across an active manuscript spread and a working AI Lore Sentinel detecting continuity contradictions—before asking them to register.

### OWN-WORLD
Celestial Marble aesthetic in deep cosmic night (`#0F111A`) with Chrysos Brilliant Gold (`#E8D08D`) illumination, Starry Slate borders (`#4A4657`), Starlight White typography (`#F2EFE9`), and mineral entity ribbons (Character Gold `#C2A878`, Location Teal `#1C2B2D`, Faction Crimson `#8B1E1E`). Framed in austere double-lined `.tome-border` panels, fine hairline star-chart dividers, and rotated diamond emblems.

### STORY
The visitor immediately encounters a living masterwork in progress. They understand that EPOS is not a cold corporate database, but an enchanted digital tome that safeguards story canon. They test the live interactive manuscript by hovering and clicking auto-linked entities, see how relations populate organically, trigger the Lore Sentinel, and proceed to "Buka Lembaran Epos" (Register) or "Akses Ingatan" (Login).

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Top navigation with celestial diamond mark, theme toggle, and sacred access button. Below, monumental all-caps Cinzel headline ("EPOS / KITAB SEMESTA") paired with authorial subtext, commanding the center. Dominating the viewport below is the Scriptorium Desk: a live interactive manuscript leaf displaying an excerpt from an epic chronicle with glowing clickable `@mentions` linking into a live mini constellation preview on the right, and the primary ceremonial CTA ("Buka Lembaran Epos") anchored with a shining gold jewel.
